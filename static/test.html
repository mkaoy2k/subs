Daily Itinerary

Day 1 (May 7) - Arrival in Kyoto

Evening: Arrive at KIX, take Haruka train to Kyoto

Check-in: Miyako Hotel Kyoto Hachijo

Travel Tip: Visit 7-11 across the street for essentials

Day 2 (May 8) - Kyoto: Fushimi Inari & Imperial Palace

Morning: Fushimi Inari Taisha (early to avoid crowds)

Lunch: Katsukura (Pork cutlet)

Afternoon: Nishiki Market, Kyoto Imperial Palace

Dinner: 京の焼肉処 弘 (Kyoto station)

Must try: 丹波屋 (sweet mochi), 生麩專門店 (fresh wheat gluten)

Day 3 (May 9) - Kyoto: Temples & Streets

Morning: Kiyomizu-dera, Sannenzaka, Ninenzaka

Lunch: 牛カツ京都勝牛 (Beef cutlet)

Afternoon: Yasaka Shrine, Hanamikoji Street

Dinner: Ryoriya Otaya (Kyoto-style kaiseki)

Dessert: 京ばあむ (sponge cake), 京阿弥 (cream puff)

Day 4 (May 10) - Arashiyama & Golden Pavilion

Morning:

  • Take JR San-in Line to Arashiyama (Platform 32/33 at Kyoto Station)
  • Visit 祐斎亭 (Yusai-tei)
  • Walk through 嵐山竹林小徑 (Arashiyama Bamboo Forest)
  • Explore 渡月橋周邊 (Togetsukyo Bridge area)
  • Try 嵯峨野可樂餅 (Sagano Korokke) near the bridge

Lunch Options:

  • 嵯峨野湯 (Sagano-yu) - Try their Tofu Pasta
  • Hanana (鯛匠) - Reserve by phone, cash only (~¥4,000)

Afternoon:

  • Take bus 204/205 to 金閣寺 (Kinkaku-ji - Golden Pavilion)
  • Walk 15 mins to 北野天滿宮 (Kitano-tenmangu Shrine)
  • Take bus 203 to Shijo Kawaramachi for dinner

Dinner: 天丼まきの 京都寺町店 (Tempura rice bowl)

Dessert:

  • 本家西尾八ッ橋 嵐山店 (Yatsuhashi - Kyoto's famous sweet)
  • Yosiya 良彌 本通り店 (Fried tofu skin with cheese)
  • 京豆富 (Black & white sesame tofu ice cream)
  • 新八茶屋 (Lemon and pistachio ice cream)

Day 5 (May 11) - Nara Day Trip

Morning:

  • Take Kintetsu Limited Express from Kyoto Station (7:55 AM or 8:25 AM)
  • Arrive at Kintetsu Nara Station (near Nara Park)
  • Walk through 奈良公園 (Nara Park) - watch for deer!
  • Take bus 77 (4 stops) to 春日大社 (Kasuga Taisha)
  • Visit 東大寺 (Tōdai-ji) and 興福寺 (Kōfuku-ji)

Lunch Options:

  • 鳥貴族 (Yakitori Torikizoku) - Chain izakaya with yakitori
  • 志津香釜飯 (Shizuka Kamameshi) - Famous for kamameshi (claypot rice)
  • オテナシ食堂 (Otenashi Shokudō) - Local restaurant

Afternoon:

  • Explore more of Nara's temples and gardens
  • Try local snacks at Nara Station:
    • 大和茶大福 (Yamatocha Daifuku) - Tea-flavored mochi
    • 大佛布丁 (Daibutsu Pudding) - Famous local pudding

Evening: Return to Kyoto

Dinner Options (near Kyoto Station):

  • Kitada Clam Soup Ramen
  • すし酒場 さしす (Sushi Bar Sashisu)

Travel Tips:

  • Use ICOCA card for Kintetsu trains (additional fare needed for Limited Express)
  • Consider purchasing a Nara 1-day bus pass if planning to use buses frequently
  • Try the local specialty: kakinoha-zushi (persimmon leaf sushi)

Day 6 (May 12) - Kyoto Free Day

Morning:

  • Leisurely morning exploring Kyoto Station area
  • Purchase JR Haruka ticket from Kyoto to Osaka for tomorrow

Kyoto Station Area Options:

  • Breakfast/Lunch:
    • 鮨徳 (Sushi Toku) - Sushi restaurant
    • Kitada Clam Soup Ramen (opens at 7 AM)
    • 拉麵小路 (Ramen Kōji) - Ramen food court with multiple shops
  • Shopping at Aeon Mall:
    • Supermarket for fresh sashimi, cakes, and fruits
    • GRANDIR bakery
    • SIZUYA bakery
    • Antenor (boutique shop)

Kawaramachi Area (河原町駅 4-chome):

  • 天丼まきの 京都寺町店 - Tempura rice bowl
  • Iroha Kitaten - Sukiyaki restaurant (reservation recommended)
  • 肉の隠れ家 - Wagyu beef restaurant in Gion
  • 挽肉と米 (Hikiniku to Gohan) - Popular rice bowl spot (arrive by 8:15 AM for 11:30 AM ticket)

Kyoto Chain Restaurants (various locations):

  • 京都勝牛 - Fried beef cutlet (main branch in Sanjo, Gion Yasaka branch)
  • 鳥貴族 - Yakitori chain
  • 名代豬排 Katsu Kura - Pork cutlet
  • 麺屋 猪一 (Menya Inoichi) - Ramen
  • HARBS - Famous for cakes (also in Osaka)
  • 敘敘苑 - Yakiniku (also in Osaka)

Kyoto Souvenirs:

  • GODIVA八橋 - Matcha and chocolate sweets
  • KyoFrance - Matcha Financier cakes
  • Sponge cake Baumkuchen
  • 祇園辻利抹茶捲心餅乾 - Matcha cream cookies

Evening:

  • Last night in Kyoto - consider revisiting favorite spots
  • Pack and prepare for tomorrow's move to Osaka

Travel Tips:

  • Charge all devices and prepare day bag for tomorrow's travel to Osaka
  • Consider using luggage forwarding service to Osaka hotel if you have heavy bags

Day 7 (May 13) - Kyoto to Osaka

Morning:

  • Check out from Miyako Hotel Kyoto Hachijo
  • Breakfast at Kyoto Station area
  • Last-minute shopping or sightseeing in Kyoto

Afternoon:

  • Travel to Osaka:
    • Option 1: Take Haruka train from Kyoto Station
    • Option 2: Take JR Tokaido-Sanyo Line Special Rapid (ICOCA accepted, ¥620, ~30 minutes)
    • Depart around 3:00 PM to arrive in Osaka by 3:30 PM
  • Check in at Hotel Hankyu RESPIRE OSAKA

Evening:

  • Exchange QR codes at Osaka Station:
    • Osaka Amazing 1-day pass at Osaka Metro Commuter Pass sales counters (Umeda Station)
    • JR Haruka ticket to Kansai Airport (KIX) for return trip
  • Window shopping in Umeda area:
    • 阪急百貨 (Hankyu Department Store) - Food hall
    • 大丸梅田 (Daimaru Umeda)
    • LUCUA1100 & LUCUA shopping malls
    • GRAND FRONT OSAKA

Dinner:

  • かに道楽 梅田 (Kani Doraku Umeda) - Crab specialty restaurant
    • Reservation recommended (3 weeks in advance)
    • Course menu around ¥12,600 per person
    • Try their grilled crab, crab sushi, and crab hot pot

Travel Tips:

  • Use luggage forwarding service (takkyubin) from Kyoto hotel to Osaka hotel if you have heavy bags
  • Purchase ICOCA card if you haven't already (works in both Kyoto and Osaka)
  • Check weather forecast and plan outfit for Osaka's slightly warmer climate
  • Charge all devices and prepare day bag for tomorrow's Osaka exploration

Evening Options (if not too tired):

  • Umeda Sky Building Floating Garden Observatory (until 10:30 PM, ¥1,500)
  • HEP FIVE Ferris Wheel (until 10:45 PM, ¥600, included with Osaka Amazing Pass)
  • Dotonbori night walk (take Midosuji Line to Namba, about 15 minutes from Umeda)

Day 8 (May 14) - Osaka Amazing Pass Day

Morning:

  • 8:30 AM - Start at Umeda Sky Building Floating Garden Observatory (opens at 9:30 AM, included with pass)
  • Take Tanimachi Line to Tanimachi 4-chome Station
  • Visit Osaka Castle (opens 9:00 AM, ¥600, included with pass)
  • Explore Nishinomaru Garden (¥200, included with pass)

Lunch: すし酒場 さしす なんばウォーク店 (Sushi Bar Sashisu)

  • Great value sushi chain with locations in Namba and Umeda
  • Try their assorted sushi platter or chef's omakase

Afternoon:

  • Take Midosuji Line to Namba Station
  • Explore Dotonbori area and take Tombori River Cruise (¥1,000, included with pass, better at night)
  • Visit Kuromon Ichiba Market (Black Door Market):
    • 西川鮮魚店 (Nishikawa Sakanaya) - Fresh seafood
    • 石橋食品 (Ishibashi Shokuhin) - Oden (Japanese hot pot)
    • 花神 (Kajin) - Seafood and Kobe beef skewers

Evening:

  • HEP FIVE Ferris Wheel (included with pass, open until 10:45 PM)
  • Dinner options in Umeda:
    • まぐろ専門店 マグロマニア (Maguro Mania) - Tuna specialty restaurant
      • Must try: Sashimi combo (¥1,539), Tamatebako set (¥1,400)
      • No reservations, arrive early
    • Negiyaki Yamamoto Umeda EST - Osaka-style savory pancakes

Day 9 (May 15) - Osaka Shopping Day

Morning:

  • Take Midosuji Line to Shinsaibashi Station
  • Explore Shinsaibashi Shopping Arcade
  • Visit Daiso (100-yen shop) for souvenirs and snacks

Lunch at Kuromon Market:

  • 鮮魚 魚豊 (Uotoyo) - Fresh seafood (opens 9:00 AM)
  • まぐろや黒銀 (Maguroya Kurogin) - Premium tuna (opens 9:00 AM)
    • Try their tuna chunks → Sashimi & Sushi set
  • 牛兆 (Gyucho) - Grilled beef skewers
  • 魚福 (Uofuku) - Baked scallop & oyster
  • 神戸屋 (Kobe-ya) - Kobe beef BBQ

Afternoon:

  • Explore Namba and Dotonbori area
  • Visit Don Quijote for tax-free shopping
  • Try street food along Dotonbori:
    • Takoyaki (octopus balls)
    • Okonomiyaki (savory pancake)
    • Kushikatsu (deep-fried skewers)

Dinner Options:

  • Ichiran Ramen Dotonbori - Famous tonkotsu ramen chain
  • Udon Ippuku - Handmade udon noodles
  • Harukoma Sushi - Affordable conveyor belt sushi

Day 10 (May 16) - Open Day / 2025 Osaka Expo

Option 1: Osaka Expo 2025

  • Purchase tickets in advance online
  • Take train to Bampaku-kinen-koen Station
  • Explore international pavilions and exhibitions
  • Try foods from around the world
  • Evening light shows and performances

Option 2: Day Trip to Kobe or Nara

  • Kobe:
    • Visit Kobe Harborland
    • Try Kobe beef for lunch
    • Nunobiki Herb Garden & Ropeway
  • Nara (if not visited earlier):
    • Todai-ji Temple and Great Buddha
    • Nara Park (deer)
    • Kasuga Taisha Shrine

Dinner Back in Osaka:

  • 木津卸売市場 (Kizu Market):
    • 魚市食堂 (Uoichi Shokudo) - Seafood donburi
    • 当志郎 (Toshiro) - Great value sushi
  • Or explore Tenma area:
    • Menroku - Duck ramen
    • Nakamuraya - Fried meatballs
    • Sushi-dokoro Maeda - Omakase (¥6,000)

Day 11 (May 17) - Departure from Osaka

Morning:

  • Check out from Hotel Hankyu RESPIRE OSAKA
  • Breakfast at 大阪駅 (Osaka Station) area
  • Last-minute shopping at Osaka Station City or KIX Airport

To Kansai Airport (KIX):

  1. From Hotel: Take elevator to 2nd floor, turn left to Yodobashi
  2. Cross sky bridge to Osaka Station
  3. At JR Osaka Station Central Gate, take escalator down
  4. Follow signs to JR Underground Gate (Haruka)
  5. Take another escalator down to Grand Front Osaka level
  6. Follow JR Underground Gate signs to ticket gates
  7. Use green ticket machine to exchange/purchase Haruka tickets
  8. Go to Platform 21 for Haruka Limited Express to KIX (47 minutes)
  9. At KIX Station, take escalator up to 2nd floor
  10. Follow signs to Terminal 1 (left after ticket gates)
  11. Check in at 4th floor for international flights

At KIX Airport:

  • Duty-free shopping (cheaper than in the city)
  • Last-minute souvenirs at Kitte Osaka (2nd floor)
  • Recommended purchases:
    • Royce' Nama Chocolate
    • Tokyo Banana
    • KitKats (Japan-exclusive flavors)
    • Butter Butler cookies

Flight: KIX → TPE (11:15 AM - 1:10 PM) → SFO (2:30 PM - 11:35 AM same day)

Travel Tips:

  • Leave hotel at least 3 hours before flight (by 8:00 AM)
  • Have passport and boarding pass ready
  • Use remaining yen at airport or exchange back to USD
  • Check all duty-free purchases are properly packed for customs
